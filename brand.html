Brand Guide

Simple, on purpose.

Servex is precise, calm and confident. These guidelines keep every touchpoint consistent across all three companies.

The mark.

The X in Servex is the symbol: two strokes crossing, one passing over the other. It stands for the point where security, technology and strategy meet, and for the companies of the group working as one.

Clear space

Keep empty space equal to the height of the X on every side of the logo.

Minimum size

16px for the app icon and 120px wide for the full logo.

Don't

Don't rotate, outline, stretch or recolor the logo, and don't place it on busy photography.

Monochrome

When only one color is available, set both strokes of the X in navy or white and keep the gap where they cross.

Color.

Navy and Servex Blue lead. Violet and orange mark Servex Digital and Servex Advisory; Servex Secure uses Servex Blue.
