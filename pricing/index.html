Questions

What happens after I pay?

You get a receipt from Stripe right away. We email you within one business day to confirm scope and schedule a kickoff.

How do monthly plans work?

Monthly plans are billed every month from the day you sign up, with no minimum term. The first month is charged at checkout together with any one-time services. Cancel anytime by emailing info@servexgroup.com; your plan stops at the end of the month you have paid for.

What if I change my mind?

If we haven't started work, you get a full refund. If work has started, we refund what you paid minus the time spent so far, at $300 per hour. See the Terms of Service.

Is payment secure?

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never touch our servers.

Can I get an invoice?

Yes. Stripe emails a receipt, and we can send a formal invoice with your company details on request.
