Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, who we share it with, and how to reach us about it.

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Who we are

Servex Group ("Servex", "we") runs servexgroup.com. For any privacy question or request, email info@servexgroup.com.

What we collect

  • What you send us: your name, email address and anything else you include when you contact us.
  • Order details: when you buy, Stripe collects your name, email, phone number and billing address and shares them with us. Your card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store them.
  • Website analytics: if you allow analytics cookies, Google Analytics records how the site is used, such as pages visited, device type and approximate location. We use it to improve the site.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes basic request data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from attacks.

Cookies and browser storage

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) are set only if you accept them. Visitors in the EU, UK and Switzerland see them switched off until they choose.
  • Your cart and your cookie choice are saved in your own browser so the site works as expected. They are not sent to us until you check out.

You can change your choice anytime with the link at the bottom of every page.

How we use your information

  • To provide the services you buy, send receipts and invoices, and support you.
  • To reply to your messages.
  • To understand and improve our website.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We send marketing emails only to people who have agreed to receive them, and every such email has an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with providers that help us run our business, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Stripe (payments)
  • Cloudflare (website hosting and security)
  • Google (website analytics, if you allow it)
  • Microsoft (business email)
  • Brevo (email sending)

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while we work with you and as long as needed for the purposes above. Payment and invoice records are kept as long as tax and accounting laws require.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it for marketing. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, including complaining to your local data protection authority. Email info@servexgroup.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, access controls and trusted providers. No system is perfectly secure, so we will tell you without undue delay if a breach affects your data.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at children.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date at the top.
